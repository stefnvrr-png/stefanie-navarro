SOP: Customer Complaint Handling
Standard Operating Procedure — drafted with Claude, structured for team training
Claude (Anthropic) SOP Writing Process Docs
My Prompt
"Help me write a Standard Operating Procedure for handling customer complaints in an e-commerce support team. Cover: acknowledging, investigating, resolving, documenting, and escalating. Format it clearly for a new team member to follow."
Department: Customer Support
Applies to all Customer Support Associates
1
Acknowledge Immediately
Respond within 1 business hour. Thank the customer, acknowledge the issue without assuming the cause. Avoid scripted-sounding language.
"I'm sorry to hear about this — let me look into it right away."
2
Gather Information
Confirm order/account details. Check internal systems first before asking the customer anything findable on your end.
3
Investigate
Review order/account history. Check with relevant teams (Logistics, Billing). Identify root cause before proposing a resolution.
4
Resolve
Offer a clear, specific resolution. Avoid vague promises. Set a timeline. Confirm the customer is satisfied.
5
Document
Log in CRM with:
  • Issue summary
  • Root cause
  • Resolution provided
  • Date resolved
6
Escalate If Needed
Escalate when: refund exceeds approval limit, customer requests a manager, or you're unsure how to resolve after investigation.
7
Follow Up
For complex cases, follow up within 48 hours to confirm the resolution worked.
Quality Standards
Acknowledge within 1 business hour · Document every resolution before closing · Maintain empathetic, professional tone · Never close without confirming customer satisfaction.
Why I Built This
I used Claude to draft the structure and language quickly, then reviewed and refined it based on real operations best practices. This is the kind of documentation I can build for any team — onboarding guides, process SOPs, or training materials — using AI to speed up the first draft while applying my own judgment to make sure it's accurate and usable.
